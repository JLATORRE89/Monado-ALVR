XR Control Panel

Headsets

Quest headsets connected over ADB (USB, or ADB over Wi‑Fi). Captures show what the wearer sees in any app.

Captures

Screenshots, recordings and uploads, stored on this PC. The Loft's Pictures and Videos show them.

Streaming

Intel XR runtime (Monado + ALVR). ALVR streams to one headset at a time.

The Intel XR runtime is not configured for this panel. Headset features still work. Set runtime_root in ~/.config/xr-control-panel/config.json to enable streaming controls.

XR app

The OpenXR app streamed to the connected headset. The Loft is a lobby of mini apps; the checkerboard is the plain stream test.
